## Everything else

- All geometry (architecture, furniture, plants, signage blanks), materials, lighting and lightmaps are generated by
  the scripts in `assets/blender/` and are original work under the repository's MIT license.
- The character (`character.glb`, its rig and animation clips) is generated by `assets/blender/modules/character.py`
  and is original work under the repository's MIT license.
- The Horizon set (`packages/web/public/horizon/`) is built by the same scripts from the same sources, with
  the Horizon palettes (`assets/blender/themes.py`); it adds no external assets.
- No Sketchfab, AI-generated (Hyper3D or similar) or third-party-branded assets are used. No logos, trademarks or
  real company names appear in any asset: signs, screens, books, boards and notebooks are blank surfaces whose
  text is drawn by the runtime.
