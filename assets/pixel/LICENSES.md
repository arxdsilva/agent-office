# Pixel art licenses

Everything in `assets/pixel/` and its build output in `packages/web/public/assets/pixel/` is **original work made
for agent-office**, released under the repository's MIT license:

- tiles, objects, characters, props, effects and UI pieces: hand-authored text pixel grids in `src/**/*.px`;
- palettes (`src/palettes.json`), including the theme colours mirrored from the 3D themes (`assets/blender/themes.py`);
- the 5×7 bitmap font (`src/font/pixel-5x7.px`).

No third-party sprites, tiles, fonts or textures are used, copied or traced (in particular nothing from Nintendo,
Game Freak or any commercial game), and no asset contains a logo, trademark or company name. Sign and screen text
is drawn at runtime with the bitmap font.
