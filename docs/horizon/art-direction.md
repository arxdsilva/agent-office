# Horizon: art direction

Horizon is the Classic office on a clear day in the near future: the **same rooms**, rebuilt in a
bright, calm palette. It should feel airy, uncluttered and quiet.

- **Surfaces:** warm whites and pearl greys, light wood, frosted and pearl glass, plants. Trim and
  metal are light grey; nothing is black except small details.
- **Shared spaces (`HORIZON_HOUSE`):** walls `#F7F7F4`, ceiling `#FBFBF9`, polished light stone floor
  `#ECEFF1`, metal `#9AA4AD`, accent soft teal `#6CCFC0`.
- **Wings (same theme ids as Classic, one soft accent each):** terracotta → peach `#F5B79A`,
  nordic → sky `#8EC9F0`, forest → sage `#A9C9A0`, ocean → mint `#7FD8C4`, neon → lilac `#B9A8F0`,
  brass → sand-gold `#E2C27A`.
- **Light lines only where they help:** cove lines under ceiling edges, wayfinding lines on Main
  Street and in the atrium, elevator frames, the Project Directory kiosk ring, the Context Hub core,
  wing signs and the status strips on desks (driven by real status). At most 1.5× white, a weak tight
  bloom, never flickering.
- **Outside:** a soft daylight sky and pale, static towers in haze.
- **People:** the Classic characters in light outfits with their wing's accent.
- **Calm by default:** the Classic HUD and panels only; name tags show for nearby agents unless you
  change it in Settings → Graphics → Advanced. No ads, dashboards, rain or traffic.
